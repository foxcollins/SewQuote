## Brand & Style

This design system elevates bespoke tailoring, haute couture pattern drafting, and custom dressmaker operations into a refined nocturnal studio environment. Adapting the warmth and tactile soul of an artisan workshop into full dark mode, the aesthetic moves away from stark, sterile digital blacks into rich charcoal wool, deep warm slates, and steamed ecru textiles.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Core
- **Nocturnal Workshop & Intimate Focus:** Built for evening fitting sessions and focused cutting-table calculations. The interface evokes late-night atelier desks illuminated by task lighting over bolts of cloth.
- **Mastery & Tactile Precision:** Retains the tactile gravity of physical materials—chalk markings, basting stitch guides, raw linen swatches, and pressed pattern paper—recast in deep, glare-free dark tones.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-fashion craft meets functional SaaS utility. Newsreader provides classical editorial authority, paired with technical monospaced metrics and high-legibility sans-serif forms.

### Visual Style
A tailored fusion of **Warm Dark Editorial** and **Artisanal Minimalist Utility**:
- **Layered Slates:** Deep warm charcoal foundations (`#161618`) layered with tailored slate containers (`#1E1E22`, `#26262B`) to achieve dimensional depth without cold blue light.
- **Basting Stitch Dividers:** Ultra-refined 1px architectural hairpins (`#333238`) that delineate spec rows like fine basting thread.
- **Warm Ecru Typography:** Ink colors shift to warm ecru (`#EDE8E1`) and muted linen (`#A8A29A`) for readable, soft contrast that eliminates eye strain during lengthy garment drafting sessions.

## Colors

The color palette adapts the atelier's material honesty into a calibrated dark system. Dark surfaces avoid achromatic pure black, instead utilizing warm charcoal undertones that preserve the tactile richness of tailored wool and dressmaker paper.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#E07148` / `#C85A32` - Terracotta Thread):** The signature vibrant terracotta accent. In dark mode, `#E07148` serves as the primary actionable focus, interactive state, and progress indicator for luminous readability against dark slates, with `#C85A32` anchoring filled buttons and container accents.
- **Secondary (`#769785` - Atelier Loden Light):** A desaturated, warm sage-moss adapted for dark mode surfaces, giving life to completed states, client identity tags, and validated garment specifications.
- **Tertiary (`#D49B6A` - Spool Amber):** Warm golden-ochre indicating active fittings, alteration notes, and pending measurements.
- **Neutral (`#161618` - Deep Charcoal Slate):** The foundational canvas tone, balancing deep pigment with subtle warmth.

### Canvas, Surface, & Border Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#161618`):** The primary view foundation representing natural dark wool cloth.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#1E1E22`):** Primary card backdrop, spec panels, list items, and table rows.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#26262B`):** Elevated modals, dropdown drawers, segmented toggle backgrounds, and hovered elements.
- **Border Refined (`#333238`):** Crisp, razor-thin structural hairpins separating cards, header sections, and form groups.
- **Border Subtle (`#27262C`):** Subordinate line dividers within nested lists and measurement metrics.

### Typography Ink Roles
- **Primary Text (`#EDE8E1` - Warm Ecru):** Delivers WCAG AAA contrast against dark surfaces while feeling warmer and softer than harsh pure white.
- **Secondary Text (`#A8A29A` - Muted Linen):** Balanced mid-tone for labels, body specifications, and garment notes.
- **Tertiary / Helper Text (`#6E6A64` - Tailor's Chalk):** Diminished tone for timestamps, minor units, and disabled inputs.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy preserves the deliberate interplay of editorial romance, functional legibility, and tabular precision. In dark mode, light weights are balanced slightly heavier where necessary to prevent visual erosion against dark slate planes.

### Font Roles
- **Newsreader:** Retains its role for all display headlines, client dossier titles, garment collection headers, and quote totals. Its editorial serif styling lends tailored heritage to the dark digital canvas.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Governs operational flow—body text, form inputs, navigation items, and client specifications. Rendered in `#EDE8E1` or `#A8A29A` to maintain crisp legibility.
- **JetBrains Mono:** Dedicated to garment measurements (chest, waist, seam allowances, ease percentages), monetary currency formats, and production timeline tracking IDs. Prevents horizontal jitter across numeric data columns.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an 8pt baseline rhythm with 4pt sub-divisions for high-density spec inputs. In dark mode, whitespace functions as intentional visual breathing room between illuminated surface tiles.

### Form Factor Behavior
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column fluid stack with strict 16px margins (`1rem`). Studio workflows anchor with a tactile bottom bar and high-contrast thumb-level buttons for fast one-handed fitting adjustments.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 6-column grid with 24px margins (`1.5rem`). Employs split-pane views pairing client measurement silhouettes directly beside fabric calculation tables.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column grid capped at 1360px max width with 40px outer margins (`2.5rem`) and 32px gutters (`2rem`). Divided into asymmetric 7/5 columns: left panel holds full garment draft details, while the right panel displays live recalculating quote tallies.

## Elevation & Depth

In dark mode, depth is conveyed through **warm tonal layering** and **refined hairline outlines** rather than diffuse light shadows, ensuring surfaces remain clean and architectural.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Grounded at `#161618`. Represents the unlit atelier cutting mat.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surface):** Filled with `#1E1E22`, encased by a 1px solid hairline border in `#333238`. No heavy shadows; edge separation is achieved through precise border contrast.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active State):** Elevated to `#26262B` with a refined 1px border `#3E3D45` and an ambient dark feather shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets, Popovers, & Drawers):** `#26262B` framed with 1px border `#3E3D45` and intense directional occlusion: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`. Background scrim employs `rgba(10, 10, 12, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft (`1`)** shape language, reflecting precision-cut pattern paper, woven atelier garment labels, and tailoring cards.

- **Containers & Cards (`0.375rem` / `6px`):** Subtle corner softenings prevent clinical harshness while preserving the architectural dignity of bespoke tailoring software.
- **Badges, Status Chips, & Action Buttons (`0.25rem` / `4px` to `0.375rem` / `6px`):** Clean, squared-off forms resembling textile tag swatches stitched into fabric.
- **Mobile Floating Trays & Drawers (`0.75rem` / `12px` top radii):** Soft, ergonomic curves tailored for tactile interaction on mobile devices.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Terracotta):** Solid `#C85A32` fill, text `#EDE8E1` in `label-md`. Border radius `6px`. Hover transitions to `#E07148` with a subtle inner edge highlight: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Touch target: 44px minimum height.
- **Secondary (Dark Atelier Slate):** Background `#1E1E22`, 1px solid border `#333238`, text `#EDE8E1`. Hover transitions border to `#E07148` and surface to `#26262B`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#E07148`. Hover applies `#1E1E22` fill with text in `#EDE8E1`.
- **Quick Measurement Dial:** Floating circular button (52px diameter) in `#C85A32` with `#EDE8E1` icon, framed by 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.

### 2. Form Inputs & Measurement Entry
- **Measurement Field:** Height 44px, surface fill `#161618`, 1px border `#333238`. Text in `metric-md` (`#EDE8E1`) aligned right. Integrated unit indicator (`in` / `cm`) in `label-sm` (`#6E6A64`).
- **Focus State:** 1.5px solid `#E07148` border with a subtle 2px soft ring: `0 0 0 2px rgba(224, 113, 72, 0.15)`.
- **Segmented Spec Selector:** Background track `#161618` with 1px border `#333238`. Active item rises to `#26262B` with `#EDE8E1` text and 1px border `#3E3D45`.

### 3. Cards & Quotation Modules
- **Garment Spec Card:** Background `#1E1E22`, 1px border `#333238`. Contains client monogram token, garment status badge, fitting countdown, and progress line.
- **Quotation Breakdown Table:** Segmented rows separated by hairline border `#27262C`. Unit costs and hourly labor rates displayed in `metric-md` (`#EDE8E1`). The summary footer card is rendered in `#26262B` with final totals highlighted in `#E07148`.

### 4. Chips & Status Badges
- Woven-label aesthetic with 4px border radius, 1px perimeter border, and uppercase `label-sm` text:
  - *Drafting:* Background `#26262B`, border `#333238`, text `#A8A29A`.
  - *Fitting Scheduled:* Background `#2B2218`, border `rgba(212, 155, 106, 0.3)`, text `#E4A76C`.
  - *Completed / Approved:* Background `#1C2921`, border `rgba(118, 151, 133, 0.3)`, text `#8EC5A6`.
  - *Rush Spec:* Background `#2E1A1B`, border `rgba(226, 120, 120, 0.35)`, text `#E27878` with a dashed basting-stitch outline.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Control Box:** 18x18px square (checkbox: 3px radius, radio: 50% circle). Border: 1.5px solid `#333238`, surface `#161618`.
- **Selected State:** Fill `#C85A32`, border `#E07148`, checkmark / pip in `#EDE8E1`.

### 6. Production Timeline & Fitting Tracker
- **Vertical Basting Stepper:** Vertical 1.5px dashed border (`#333238`) linking sequence nodes. Completed fittings appear as solid `#769785` dots with checkmarks. The active fitting step is highlighted with a 10px `#E07148` disc encased in a 2px `#26262B` gap and a subtle glowing ring.

### 7. Nocturnal Bottom Navigation Bar
- Fixed mobile navigation in `#161618` at 92% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)` and a top border 1px solid `#333238`.
- Height: 64px + safe area padding. Active tab icon and label illuminate in `#E07148`, inactive tabs remain in `#6E6A64`.
