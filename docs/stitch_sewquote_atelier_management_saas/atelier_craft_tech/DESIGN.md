## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intersection of bespoke tailoring craftsmanship and precise digital utility. Built specifically for independent dressmakers, bespoke tailors, and boutique fashion ateliers, the aesthetic merges the warmth and tactile refinement of a garment workshop with the crisp efficiency of modern SaaS software.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Core
- **Artisanal & Tactile:** Warm, inviting, and human. The interface honors traditional garment construction with natural cloth-like undertones rather than cold, sterile software greys.
- **Accurate & Professional:** Unwavering clarity for complex garment measurements, layered labor costs, fabric consumption, and production milestones.
- **Uncluttered & Modern:** A blend of warm minimalism and editorial typography, avoiding clutter to let pattern notes, fabric swatches, and quote figures breathe.

### Visual Style
A tailored hybrid of **Warm Editorial Minimalism** and **Refined Utility**:
- Micro-fine hairline borders reminiscent of basting stitches and dressmaker pattern chalk lines.
- Generous, warm paper/linen surface foundations paired with deep charcoal typography for maximum legibility in studio environments.
- High-density information modules optimized for quick one-handed atelier mobile use (measurement sheets, timeline trackers, fabric yield counters).

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from atelier materials: terracotta pattern weights, steamed linen canvases, tailored charcoal wool, pressed herbs, and raw brass hardware.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#C85A32` - Terracotta Thread):** Represents focal actions, primary CTAs, active timeline checkpoints, and key quote totals. Grounded, warm, and distinctive.
- **Secondary (`#2C3E35` - Atelier Loden):** A deep, tailored botanical green used for structure, client identity cards, finalized state badges, and elevated accents.
- **Tertiary (`#C98A58` - Spool Amber):** Soft, warm ochre for pending statuses, measurement flags, highlights, and secondary interactive states.
- **Neutral (`#1C1D1F` - Carbon Slate):** The primary ink color for high-contrast, razor-sharp typography and structural hairpins.

### Canvas & Surface Tokens
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF8F5` - Pressed Linen):** Replaces pure digital white with a subtle, glare-reducing warm parchment tone.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#FFFFFF` - Paper White):** Pure backdrop for input fields, interactive measurement cards, and modals.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#F3EFEA` - Raw Muslin):** Neutral container fill for table headers, segment controls, and secondary card containers.
- **Surface Border (`#E5DFD7` - Basting Line):** Delicate, low-contrast separator border keeping interfaces crisp without visual noise.

### Functional & Status Roles
- **Success / Completed (`#2D5A43` / `#EBF3EE`):** Muted Sage for finished garments and cleared invoices.
- **Warning / Alteration Needed (`#B8621B` / `#FDF3E7`):** Warm Amber for fit adjustments or pending fabric sourcing.
- **Error / Critical Alert (`#A83232` / `#FBEAEA`):** Deep Crimson for overdue deadlines or mismatched spec measurements.
- **Draft / In-Progress (`#6D5D53` / `#F0ECE9`):** Thread Taupe for unfinished draft quotations.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances couture tradition and technical operational utility through three distinct typefaces:

1. **Newsreader (Display & Headlines):** Infuses an editorial, bespoke atelier journal aesthetic. Used in client titles, high-level summary cards, and brand touchpoints.
2. **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Form Labels):** A contemporary humanist sans-serif offering warm letterforms, generous counters, and exceptional legibility across dense quote lists, customer notes, and client communication.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Technical Measurements & Currency Totals):** Fixed-width clarity for dimensional measurements (bust, waist, inseam, seam allowances), pricing breakouts, and SKU references to prevent eye-drift when reading columns of figures.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an **8pt modular baseline** (with 4pt sub-increments for compact measurement grids and micro-badges), optimized specifically for hand-held studio devices and desktop cutting-table screens.

### Grid Framework
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column fluid stack with strict 16px (`1rem`) outer canvas margin and 16px gutter. Navigation anchors permanently to an ergonomic bottom app bar with elevated action triggers.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid, 24px margins, split-view panels for real-time quotation preview beside measurement entry.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column structured grid (max-width 1360px), 40px outer margin, 32px gutters. Employs asymmetric splits: 7 columns for active fitting/spec management, 5 columns for live dynamic quotation calculations.

### Spacing Philosophy
- **Micro-intervals (`space-xs`, `space-sm`):** Reserved for technical metric clusters, such as measurement tag labels paired to inch/centimeter values.
- **Card-level padding (`space-md`, `space-lg`):** Delivers clean separation for client fit records, fabric cost cards, and multi-step tailoring workflows.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than artificial, heavy software dropshadows, depth reflects physically pressed paper cards, parchment slips, and tailor's swatch tags layered over linen.

### The Tactile Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Neutral canvas `#FAF8F5`. Default state for full-screen workflows.
- **Level 1 (Paper Surface):** `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1px solid border (`#E5DFD7`) and an ambient, warm feather shadow: `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 29, 31, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(28, 29, 31, 0.02)`. Used for fitting cards, timeline rows, and quote item blocks.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Card / Hover):** `#FFFFFF` with `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(28, 29, 31, 0.07), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(28, 29, 31, 0.03)`. Used for active line items and draggable production steps.
- **Level 3 (Modal Drawer & Sheet):** Bottom navigation sheets, client measurement popovers, and floating quick-quote trays: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(28, 29, 31, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px rgba(229, 223, 215, 0.85)`. Uses soft background backdrop filter: `backdrop-filter: blur(8px) bg-stone-900/25`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (`1`)** approach, mirroring the subtle rounded contours of fabric swatches, notched tailor patterns, and dressmaker labels. 

- **Containers & Cards (`0.375rem` / `6px`):** Subtle corner softness maintains an architectural, structured, and professional demeanor without feeling overly casual or bubble-like.
- **Badges, Tags, & Buttons (`0.25rem` / `4px` to `0.375rem` / `6px`):** Clean and squared-off with minimal corner breaks, evoking woven label tags sewn into garments.
- **Bottom Navigation Bar & Drawer Handles (`0.5rem` / `8px` top radius):** Ergonomically rounded for touch-friendly mobile handling.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Terracotta):** Solid `#C85A32` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Height: 44px (touch target minimum). Font: `label-md`. Border radius: 6px. Subtle 1px inner highlight for tactile depth.
- **Secondary (Atelier Loden Outline):** 1px solid `#2C3E35`, `#2C3E35` text, transparent background. Hover state fills `#2C3E35` with `#FAF8F5` text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border, text in `#C85A32` or `#1C1D1F`, hover shows `#F3EFEA` background.
- **Quick Action Speed-Dial:** Floating circular trigger on mobile (52px diameter) containing a needle-thread/plus icon for instantly recording client measurements or adding extra trims.

### 2. Form Inputs & Measurement Snapshots
- **Measurement Input Fields:** Single-row numeric fields featuring right-aligned monospaced values (`metric-md`) and integrated unit toggles (`in` / `cm`). Border: 1px `#E5DFD7`, surface `#FFFFFF`. Focus ring: 1.5px solid `#C85A32` without loud outer glows.
- **Pattern Spec Quick-Toggles:** Segmented selector chips (`#F3EFEA` container, active state `#FFFFFF` with hairline shadow) for silhouette type, seam finish, and zipper style.

### 3. Cards & Quotation Modules
- **Client & Garment Card:** White container, 1px border `#E5DFD7`. Contains client avatar/monogram, garment style pill, scheduled fitting date, and completion progress indicator.
- **Price Breakdown Card:** Divided into distinct sections (Fabric & Notions, Pattern Drafting, Cutting & Assembly, Hand Finishing). Each row shows unit, yardage/hours, rate, and total formatted in monospaced numerals (`metric-md`). The final total is highlighted in a `#F3EFEA` footer bar with prominent terracotta totals.

### 4. Badges & Status Tags
- Fabric-inspired rectangular tags with 4px border radius and uppercase `label-sm` monospaced lettering:
  - *Drafting:* `#F0ECE9` background, `#6D5D53` text.
  - *First Fitting:* `#FDF3E7` background, `#B8621B` text.
  - *Bespoke Approved:* `#EBF3EE` background, `#2D5A43` text.
  - *Rush Order:* `#FBEAEA` background, `#A83232` text with a subtle dashed outline mimicking red basting thread.

### 5. Production Timeline & Fitting Tracker
- **Vertical Fitting Stepper (Mobile):** Thin 1.5px vertical basting line connecting circular step indicators. Completed checkpoints turn solid `#2C3E35` with checkmarks; the active fitting step is signaled by a ringed `#C85A32` dot with pulse indicator.

### 6. Mobile Studio Bottom Navigation Bar
- Grounded, frosted bar fixed to the bottom viewport (`#FAF8F5` at 92% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and top border 1px solid `#E5DFD7`).
- Dedicated 4-item tab structure: *Quotes*, *Fittings*, *New Spec (Center Accent)*, *Atelier Clients*, *Settings*. Height: 64px + safe area padding.
